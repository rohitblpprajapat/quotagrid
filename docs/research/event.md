### Fields on the Blitz Submission Form
1. **Project Image** (`id="submit-image"` / `id="edit-image"`):
   * Label: `Upload a project image *`
   * Requirements: `PNG, JPG, or GIF up to 4 MB`
   * Mandatory on initial submission.
2. **Project Title** (`name="title"`, `id="submit-title"`):
   * Label: `Project Title *`
   * Placeholder: `Enter your project title`
   * Type: text, Required.
3. **Category** (`name="category"`, `id="submit-category"`):
   * Label: `Category *`
   * Placeholder: `e.g., DeFi, NFT`
   * Status: **Active/Required** (`collect_category: true` in live event configuration).
4. **Description** (`name="description"`, `id="submit-description"`):
   * Label: `Description *`
   * Placeholder: `Describe your project, what it does, and what makes it special...`
   * Type: textarea (4 rows), Required.
5. **Team** (`fieldset` Team):
   * Displays team members from the *Form Team* tab (`owner` labeled with `(Owner)`). Team management occurs via the event portal team formation feature.
6. **Tweet URL** (`name="tweet_url"`, `id="submit-tweet"`):
   * Label: `Tweet URL`
   * Placeholder: `https://x.com/yourhandle/status/... (optional)`
   * Status: **Active** (`collect_tweet_url: true` in live event configuration), Optional.
7. **GitHub or GitLab URL** (`name="github_url"`, `id="submit-github"`):
   * Label: `GitHub or GitLab URL *`
   * Placeholder: `https://github.com/user/repo or https://gitlab.com/user/repo`
   * Type: url, Required.
8. **Demo URL** (`name="demo_url"`, `id="submit-demo"`):
   * Label: `Demo URL *`
   * Placeholder: `https://your-demo.com`
   * Explanatory text: *"If you don't have a demo URL, enter your repository link again."*
   * Type: url, Required.
9. **Pitch Video** (`video_instructions`):
   * Status: **NOT REQUIRED / NOT VERIFIED for this event** (`video_instructions: null`, `submission_mode: "live"`). Presentations are delivered live in 3-minute slots.

## 8. Full Idea Board List (15 Ideas)

* **Source URL:** Notion Idea Board Page (`bf363675-94f2-8340-b11a-81608e882647`): [`https://monad-foundation.notion.site/Idea-Board-bf36367594f28340b11a81608e882647`](https://monad-foundation.notion.site/Idea-Board-bf36367594f28340b11a81608e882647)
* **Guideline:** *"These are starting points, not requirements. Build whatever you want - this list exists so nobody loses their first hour staring at an empty repo. Every idea here passed the same test: can 3 people ship a working on-chain demo in 6 hours, and does it still make sense as a company a year from now?"*

1. **Prediction markets for outcomes with no existing venue**  
   *AI model benchmarks, content virality thresholds, cultural tipping points, emerging market indicators — things nobody currently lets you trade.*
2. **Per-second salary streaming with idle yield**  
   *Get paid every second instead of bi-weekly, with your unspent balance earning yield in the background until you spend it.*
3. **Parametric insurance with no claims process**  
   *Cover that triggers automatically on an on-chain-verifiable event. No forms, no adjuster, instant settlement.*
4. **Payments embedded in social gestures**  
   *Splitting a bill, sending a gift, tipping a creator — where the money moves like a message, not a transaction.*
5. **Commitment contracts and accountability pools**  
   *Put real money behind a behavioural goal — screen time, step count, quitting a habit — verified by device-native signals. Those who fail fund the rewards of those who follow through.*
6. **Programmable gifts that unlock on conditions**  
   *"Here's $500 when you graduate." "Here's your share when the company exits." Financial commitments that execute without anyone having to remember or chase.*
7. **Attention futures**  
   *Stake on the content or creators you believe will break through, before the crowd gets there. Earn if you called it early.*
8. **Early-supporter registries**  
   *Cryptographic proof you were there before the audience arrived, with financial rewards attached to being early.*
9. **TCG marketplace with real-time onchain order books**  
   *Transparent price discovery, instant settlement, and none of the 10–15% cut incumbent platforms take on every trade.*
10. **Rental markets for high-value cards and assets**  
    *Competitive players get access without the capital outlay; collectors earn yield on assets sitting idle.*
11. **Generative art that evolves**  
    *Pieces whose visual traits or behaviour change based on on-chain events, holder actions, community votes, or oracle-fed real-world data. A living object, not a static collectible.*
12. **Mobile-native proof of personhood using passkeys**  
    *No invasive biometrics, no centralised issuer — verified in a single tap on hardware people already own (uses native P256 precompile 0x0100 / EIP-7951).*
13. **Content passports for AI-generated assets**  
    *Every generated asset carries a cryptographic certificate of origin and a tamper-evident audit trail that survives moving between platforms.*
14. **Cross-application AI memory**  
    *A user-owned context layer that persists across products, so your assistant isn't amnesiac every time you switch apps.*
15. **Decentralised data labelling with onchain micropayments**  
    *Contributors earn stablecoin rewards for annotating robotics and sensor data, paid per task, which makes global participation practical at scale.*
